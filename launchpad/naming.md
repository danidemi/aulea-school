## Recommendation

Top pick: **Aulea**.
- Short, easy to say in Italian and English.
- "Aula" means classroom, which fits live quizzes and trainer-led courses.
- It does not limit the product to quizzes, decks, or self-paced learning.
- .io, .ai and .academy are free. The .com and .it are taken, so you may need to buy them or use a variant.

Runner-up: **Syllabo**.
- The .it is free.
- It fits course planning well.
- Risk: an open-source AI course-planning project has the same name. The name is also narrower.

Also viable: **Formaly**. No collisions found, but it is the least meaningful of the three, and the .com and .it are taken.

## Before you commit

1. Run the chosen name through EUIPO TMview and the UIBM database (classes 9, 41, 42).
2. Check whether the taken domains are parked or for sale.
3. Say the name aloud to a few trainers and HR contacts. Check how they spell it back.
