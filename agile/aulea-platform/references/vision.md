# VISION

`aulea-platform` will be a tool part of the larger `learning-toolkit` that will support trainers and students along the whole didactic process i.e: collecting course requirements, course design, material preparation, assessment. It will help in classical trainer lead courses, self paced courses, and Assigned Self-Paced Learning, during which HR plan what self paced courses the employee should follow.

## MAIN ARCHITECTURE

The trainer will use an AI agent locally to produce the didactic material, that will be assembled in one or more logical packages, i.e.: the deck package, quizzes and so on. `aulea-platform` will offer supporting tool, as management of interactive quizzes for the whole class, download of slides.
